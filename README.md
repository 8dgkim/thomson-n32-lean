# The Thomson problem for N = 32: a Lean 4 proof

Among thirty-two distinct points on the unit sphere in ℝ³, the twelve vertices of a regular icosahedron together with the twenty vertices of its dual dodecahedron minimize the Coulomb energy Σ_{i<j} 1/‖x_i − x_j‖, and they are the only minimizer up to rotations, reflections, and relabeling. This repository contains a Lean 4 proof of that statement, a paper describing the proof, and the scripts and records of its verification.

Found by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), run at high reasoning effort in Claude Code, under the direction of Kim DongGyu, between October 23 and November 4, 2026. The proof, the Lean code, the certificate data, and the paper were produced by Claude Fable 5.1. Kim DongGyu directed the sessions, audited the formal statement, and publishes this work.

Checked by the Lean 4 kernel; not yet reviewed by experts.

## The theorem

The statement is the module `Thomson32.Statement`, which imports only Mathlib:

```lean
def ThomsonThirtyTwo : Prop :=
  ∀ x : Fin 32 → R3, (∀ i, ‖x i‖ = 1) → Function.Injective x →
    energy candidate ≤ energy x ∧
    (energy x = energy candidate ↔
      ∃ (g : R3 ≃ₗᵢ[ℝ] R3) (σ : Equiv.Perm (Fin 32)), ∀ i, x i = g (candidate (σ i)))
```

The final theorem is `theorem thomson_thirty_two : ThomsonThirtyTwo` in `Thomson32.Proof`, and `#print axioms thomson_thirty_two` reports `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

## How the proof works

1. **A three-point bound** in the sense of Cohn and Woo, given by an exact degree-16 certificate, bounds the energy of every configuration from below. It falls short of the candidate's energy by less than 4·10⁻⁴², which forces every inner product of a configuration with energy at most the candidate's to within 10⁻¹⁰ of one of the candidate's eleven values.
2. **A window classification** shows that the only way to fill those windows is the candidate's own pattern, up to relabeling.
3. **Local rigidity**, through an exact certificate for the Hessian, shows that a configuration with that pattern and energy at most the candidate's is the candidate itself, up to rotation and reflection.

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) and the paper give the details.
